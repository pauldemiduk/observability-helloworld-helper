# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of **Hello World Microservices** pledge to make participation in our project and community a **welcoming, inclusive, and respectful** experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of **positive behavior** that we encourage:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Being constructive in feedback and discussions
- Accepting responsibility and gracefully handling mistakes
- Using inclusive and welcoming language

Examples of **unacceptable behavior** include:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes/comments
- Publishing private information without consent
- Personal or political attacks
- Unwanted sexual attention or advances
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers have the right to **remove, edit, or reject** comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeat offenders may be temporarily or permanently banned.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it via **[email/maintainers contact]**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated confidentially.
